About

Alvaro's key responsibility is to develop educational programming, provide outreach, and hold consultations on a variety of topics related to media and maker projects at UCR.

Alvaro earned his BS in Computer Science and Engineering and his MBA in Organizational Leadership and Information Technology, both from the University of La Verne. He joined the library in 2020.
